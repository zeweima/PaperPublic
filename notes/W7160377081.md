# [An Interpretable Physics‐Guided Deep Learning (IPGDL) Framework for Blue‐Green Water Robust Projection and Nonlinear Interpretation in the River Basin](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr041535)

**Authors:** Zhonghui Guo, Chang Feng, Qing Liu et al.
**Venue:** Water Resources Research · **Date:** 2026-05-01 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr041535
**Score:** 9/10<sup> top pick</sup>
**Source:** abstract

## TL;DR

A physics-guided deep learning framework combining SWAT with CNN-BiLSTM and SHAP projects blue-green water to 2100 in the Xiangjiang River Basin, with low-forcing scenarios producing the most extreme responses.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Long-term projection of blue water (BW) and green water (GW) resources requires capturing nonlinear dynamics, but purely physics-based models (SWAT) and purely data-driven models each have limitations in complex multi-decadal projections with interpretability.
- **Method:** IPGDL framework couples SWAT's physical constraints with a CVOA-optimized CNN-BiLSTM (CCB) network; SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations) provides global, interaction, time-step, and spatial interpretation. Driven by quality-assured CMIP6 climate and PLUS land-use scenarios for the Xiangjiang River Basin (XRB), China; projection period 2022–2100, historical baseline 1991–2020.
- **Key result:** Meteorological features dominate BW and GW variability (46% and 53% SHAP importance respectively); critical precipitation thresholds identified at 140.5 mm (BW) and 109.4 mm (GW). Low-forcing scenarios trigger the largest responses: −43.6% BW and +34.8% GW with smaller uncertainty than medium/high-forcing scenarios.
- **Why it matters:** Demonstrates that embedding physical process knowledge into deep learning reduces projection bias while SHAP enables mechanistic interpretation of nonlinear threshold effects, temporal lags, and spatial controls — a transferable framework for watershed management under climate change.
- **Caveats:** Applied to a single basin (XRB); generalizability to other hydroclimatic regimes unverified. SWAT parameterization uncertainty propagates into the physics-guided component. Counter-intuitive result (lower forcing → larger extremes) warrants scrutiny across other basins.
