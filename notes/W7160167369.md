## TL;DR

Multi-year field data show combined mineral–organic fertilization improves productivity, soil C, and system resilience in a dryland pearl millet–mustard–cowpea rotation.

## Summary
- **Problem:** Whether integrated mineral–organic nutrient management sustains yields, builds soil organic carbon, and confers resilience in semi-arid legume-cereal rotations across multiple seasons.
- **Method:** Multi-year field observations on pearl millet–mustard–cowpea cropping systems comparing mineral-only, organic-only, and combined mineral–organic fertilization treatments; soil carbon dynamics tracked over time.
