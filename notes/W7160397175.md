## TL;DR

Global meta-analysis quantifies SOC gains from legume-inclusion cropping systems and maps regional suitability for adoption.

## Summary
- **Problem:** Legume-inclusive diversified cropping systems (intercropping, rotation) are promoted for SOC sequestration, but the magnitude of effect and geographic suitability for deployment are poorly constrained globally.
- **Method:** Global meta-analysis of published field experiments comparing legume-inclusion systems vs. conventional monocultures; paired with a regional suitability simulation to identify where these systems most effectively build SOC.
