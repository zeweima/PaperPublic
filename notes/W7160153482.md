# [A Hybrid Biophysical‐Machine Learning Framework for Diurnal Surface Energy Flux Estimation Using Proximal Sensing](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr041510)

**Authors:** James F. Cross, Kanishka Mallick, Guler (Rojda) Aslan-Sungur et al.
**Venue:** Water Resources Research · **Date:** 2026-05-01 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr041510
**Score:** 9/10<sup> top pick</sup>
**Source:** abstract

## TL;DR

Hybrid STIC1.2 + ML model estimates sub-hourly LE and H over four agricultural systems at R² 0.81–0.94 and 0.46–0.72 without requiring net radiation or soil heat flux observations.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Thermal IR-based surface energy balance models (e.g., STIC1.2) require net radiation and soil heat flux inputs that are unavailable in data-sparse agricultural settings; satellite revisit limits diurnal coverage.
- **Method:** STIC1.2 biophysical model extended to sub-hourly resolution via proximal (ground-based) thermal sensing over multiple growing seasons at four distinct agricultural systems; ML component added to predict surface available energy, replacing the need for observed net radiation and soil heat flux; explainable ML (SHAP-based exML) applied post-hoc to attribute residual errors.
- **Key result:** Hybrid model achieves R² = 0.81–0.94 for latent energy (LE) and R² = 0.46–0.72 for sensible heat (H) across all four systems; exML diagnoses error sources tied to unrepresented environmental drivers.
- **Why it matters:** Enables continuous diurnal ET estimation from minimal sensor networks in data-limited agricultural landscapes; exML framework provides a principled path for iterative model improvement and variable selection.
- **Caveats:** Tested only on agricultural systems; H performance (R² as low as 0.46) lags LE; generalizability to non-agricultural or data-rich satellite contexts not demonstrated; proximal sensing infrastructure still required.
