# [Capturing Spatiotemporal and Subgrid Variability in Global Land Surface Albedo Parameterization](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025ms005436)

**Authors:** Akarsh Ralhan, Xin‐Zhong Liang
**Venue:** JAMES (J. Adv. Modeling Earth Systems) · **Date:** 2026-05-01 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2025ms005436

## TL;DR

Physics-informed ML parameterization trained on 19 years of MODIS BRDF data achieves R²=0.88 for global land surface albedo, outperforming static look-up tables by capturing spatiotemporal and subgrid variability.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Existing albedo schemes in Earth system models rely on static look-up tables or semi-empirical formulations that miss spatiotemporal dynamics and subgrid heterogeneity, degrading energy balance simulations.
- **Method:** ML parameterization trained on 2003–2021 MODIS BRDF data (19 years) using 10 biogeophysical predictors (solar geometry, LAI, soil moisture, soil texture, topography, background climate) to predict direct/diffuse albedo in VIS and NIR bands across major land-cover categories. A novel Surface Albedo Localization Factor (SALF) corrects for subgrid heterogeneity as a static additive component.
- **Key result:** Overall R²=0.88, MAPE=7.5% against MODIS albedo; band-specific R² ranges from 0.85 (direct VIS) to 0.90 (diffuse NIR). SALF alone contributes an average R² increase of 0.16 and MAPE reduction of 5.1%. Solar zenith angle and LAI dominate dynamic variability; soil texture and topography govern static variability.
- **Why it matters:** Provides a physically grounded, interpretable alternative to empirical schemes for integration into land surface and Earth system models; improves albedo representation for challenging land covers (barren, shrublands) where existing methods fail.
- **Caveats:** Trained on MODIS record; performance in data-sparse regions or land-cover transitions not fully evaluated. Subgrid SALF is static, potentially missing dynamic subgrid changes (e.g., post-disturbance recovery).
