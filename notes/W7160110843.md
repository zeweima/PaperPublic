## TL;DR

Improved land management in semi-arid grasslands reduces net CO₂ losses, quantified via portable chamber measurements upscaled across the landscape.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Semi-arid grasslands are vulnerable carbon sources; whether improved management practices can meaningfully reduce CO₂ losses and by how much remains unclear.
- **Method:** Portable chamber CO₂ flux measurements upscaled to landscape scale across semi-arid grassland sites under contrasting management regimes; analysis of net ecosystem carbon balance.
- **Key result:** <unclear from abstract>
- **Why it matters:** Demonstrates that management interventions (grazing regime, vegetation restoration, etc.) can shift semi-arid grasslands toward reduced carbon losses, informing climate-smart land management policy in dryland regions.
- **Caveats:** Portable chamber upscaling introduces spatial uncertainty; semi-arid site conditions may limit generalizability to wetter or more degraded grassland systems.
