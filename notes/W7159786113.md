# [Quantifying the Rapid Propagation of Rainfall and Evapotranspiration Signals Into Soils](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026GL121730)

**Authors:** Huibin Gao, Harsh Beria, Marius Floriancic et al.
**Venue:** Geophysical Research Letters · **Date:** 2026 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2026GL121730
**Score:** <unclear from abstract>/10
**Source:** full text

## TL;DR

ET signals propagate into soil with near-mirror symmetry to precipitation but decay 3–6x slower, especially at forested sites where root vascular lags dominate.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Precipitation's downward propagation through soil columns is well studied, but whether ET generates an analogous (opposite-sign) signal at depth has not been quantified. Jointly estimating both is hard because P and ET signals are overprinted in soil moisture records.
- **Method:** Ensemble Rainfall-Runoff Analysis (ERRA) — a nonparametric deconvolution and de-mixing approach — applied to hourly soil moisture (VWC at 4 depths, 5–60 cm), precipitation, and eddy-covariance ET at 4 Swiss sites (2 grasslands: Rietholzbach, Chamau; 2 forests: Laegern, Davos) spanning 5–18 yr of data; May–October only to exclude snowmelt.
- **Key result:** (1) P and ET produce roughly equal-magnitude but opposite-sign soil water recharge responses at all depths; (2) P response peaks within 1 hr and ceases within 1–2 hr; ET response persists ~3 hr at grasslands and 5–6 hr at forests; (3) peak recharge response to P is damped 81–96% by the deepest sensor (30–60 cm) with negligible lag; (4) topsoil (5 cm) SWR perturbations propagate to 30–60 cm in <1 hr with ~90% peak damping and net recharge fractions of ~5% at grassland sites and ~15% at forest sites reaching deepest depths.
- **Why it matters:** Provides the first field-based, empirically de-mixed impulse response functions for both P and ET forcing of soil moisture, offering direct constraints for testing LSMs and soil-plant-atmosphere continuum models. The ERRA framework is extensible to link soil moisture responses to groundwater and streamflow.
- **Caveats:** Proof-of-concept limited to 4 Swiss sites; point-scale soil moisture sensors may not be representative (high spatial heterogeneity noted). Signal interpretations assume linearity. Net recharge metric cannot distinguish between reduced infiltration flux and increased preferential flow at depth. Analysis restricted to shallow soils (≤60 cm) and the May–October growing season.
