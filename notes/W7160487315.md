# [Historical and Future Global Irrigation Energy Consumption by Fuel and Region](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025ef007970)

**Authors:** Ying Zhang, Steven J. Smith, Kelly Casper
**Venue:** Earth's Future · **Date:** 2026-05-01 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2025ef007970
**Score:** 8/10<sup> top pick</sup>
**Source:** abstract

## TL;DR

First country-level dataset of irrigation energy consumption by fuel type reveals a projected global shift toward solar pumping, with India and Pakistan leading, while the USA and China remain grid-electricity dominant.

## Summary

- **Problem:** No comprehensive global dataset of irrigation energy consumption broken down by fuel type (electric, diesel, solar) existed, limiting understanding of the food-energy-water nexus and emissions intensity of irrigated agriculture.
- **Method:** Literature review used to develop a logistic-transformed regression model estimating fuel-source shares across countries historically; country-level irrigation energy consumption dataset constructed and cross-validated against IEA agricultural energy statistics; projections run through the Global Change Analysis Model (GCAM) multisector dynamics model under a reference scenario.
- **Key result:** Under the reference scenario, a global shift away from diesel toward solar irrigation pumping is projected; India and Pakistan lead solar adoption while the USA and China continue to rely primarily on grid electricity; regional patterns vary substantially.
- **Why it matters:** Irrigation energy is a significant fraction of agricultural production costs and emissions; this dataset enables integrated food-energy-water modeling and can inform electrification and decarbonization strategies in the agricultural sector.
- **Caveats:** Regression-based fuel-share estimates carry uncertainty where primary data are sparse; the reference scenario projection does not capture policy-driven transitions; solar cost trajectories and electrification rates may diverge from GCAM assumptions.
