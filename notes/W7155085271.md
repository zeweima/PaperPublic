# [On-device artificial intelligence agent based on language models for electrochemical water desalination](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2026.125995)

**Authors:** Zahid Ullah, Hoo Hugo Kim, Moon Son
**Venue:** Water Research · **Date:** 2026-04-21 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2026.125995
**Score:** 9/10<sup> top pick</sup>
**Source:** abstract

## TL;DR

An edge-deployed LLM platform for electrochemical desalination cuts hallucination 60% and raises applied-current prediction R² from 0.03 to 0.63 without cloud connectivity.

## Summary
- **Problem:** Optimizing electrochemical water treatment requires domain expertise and compute unavailable at many decentralized facilities; cloud-based AI raises connectivity and privacy concerns.
- **Method:** On-device LLM (runs on Raspberry Pi) fused with electrochemical process knowledge; validated against 320 published studies; tested on effluent concentration, energy consumption, and applied current prediction with incomplete sensor inputs.
- **Key result:** Hallucination rate reduced 60%; predictive R² > 0.80 for most variables; applied current R² improved from 0.03 to 0.63.
- **Why it matters:** Demonstrates viable edge-AI deployment for water treatment in resource-limited settings where physics-based models cannot run and cloud access is absent.
- **Caveats:** Validation is literature-based (320 studies) rather than prospective field trials; generalizability across diverse water chemistries and electrochemical cell designs is unclear from abstract.
