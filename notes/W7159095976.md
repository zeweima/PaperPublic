## TL;DR

Fire smoke is a growing driver of US ozone-related mortality, accounting for 61.5% of the relevant burden by 2023.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Wildfire smoke is known to worsen PM2.5 air quality, but its contribution to ozone pollution and associated mortality in the US is poorly quantified and likely growing.
- **Method:** <unclear from abstract> — study presumably uses atmospheric chemistry modeling or observational analysis to attribute ozone concentrations and mortality to fire smoke over a multi-year record ending in 2023.
