## TL;DR

Focus issue editorial synthesizing multi-scale N science linking food systems, N₂O emissions, and climate mitigation via integrated monitoring, modeling, and governance.

## Summary
- **Problem:** Nitrogen overuse drives environmental degradation and climate forcing (N₂O), yet measurement, modeling, and cross-sectoral governance remain fragmented across scales.
- **Method:** Editorial synthesis of an ERL focus issue; covers subnational to global N assessments, denitrification/N₂O modeling uncertainties, emerging monitoring networks (SmartField, canN₂Onet, N₂Onet), scenario analyses of agro-food systems, and nutrient recycling options.
- **Key result:** Scenario analyses show substantial N-loss reductions achievable through dietary shifts, enhanced circularity, and system integration; human-excreta N recovery identified as underexplored lever; economic trade-offs of global N-use policy examined across contributions.
- **Why it matters:** Provides a programmatic framework for reconciling food production with N₂O mitigation—highlights where harmonized monitoring and robust N-budget accounting are most needed.
- **Caveats:** Editorial/overview paper; quantitative synthesis numbers are distributed across cited studies rather than directly reported here; findings depend on quality of constituent contributions.
