# [Mechanistic Microbial Death Pathways Improve Regional Soil Carbon Simulations across Diverse Cropland Ecosystems](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.5c16652)

## TL;DR

Incorporating mechanistic microbial death pathways into soil carbon models improves regional SOC simulations across diverse cropland ecosystems.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Standard soil carbon models treat microbial death as a simple first-order process, which may misrepresent SOC dynamics across heterogeneous croplands at regional scale.
- **Method:** A microbial soil carbon model with mechanistic death pathways (distinguishing starvation, predation, and other mortality routes) was applied across diverse cropland ecosystems; evaluated at regional scale against observational benchmarks.
- **Key result:** <unclear from abstract>
- **Why it matters:** Explicit microbial death mechanisms constrain a key source of uncertainty in land-surface and biogeochemical models; better regional SOC prediction is critical for carbon accounting in agricultural soils.
- **Caveats:** Scope limited to croplands; parameterization of microbial death rates may not transfer to natural ecosystems; full spatial extent and validation metrics unclear from available metadata.
