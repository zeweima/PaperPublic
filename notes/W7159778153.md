# [Sources of Uncertainty in Ocean Net Primary Productivity Projections Under Climate Change](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025GL119652)

**Authors:** Natacha Le Grix, Alessandro Tagliabue
**Venue:** Geophysical Research Letters · **Date:** 2026 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2025GL119652
**Score:** <unclear from abstract>/10
**Source:** full text

## TL;DR

Preindustrial NPP state dominates absolute projection uncertainty throughout 1850–2100; NPPA uncertainty doubles by 2100 driven by divergent ESM shifts in decadal mean (low-mid latitudes) and seasonal cycle (high latitudes).

## Summary

- **Problem:** CMIP6 ESMs diverge strongly on ocean NPP projections, but the specific sources of that uncertainty — and whether they are reducible — were not systematically decomposed at monthly resolution across both absolute NPP and the anomaly relative to preindustrial (NPPA).
- **Method:** Inter-model variance decomposition across 7 CMIP6 ESMs (ACCESS-ESM1-5, CMCC-ESM2, CanESM5-1, CNRM-ESM2-1, IPSL-CM6A-LR, MIROC-ES2L, UKESM1-0-LL) under SSP1-2.6 through SSP5-8.5, 1850–2100, at 1° monthly resolution. NPP decomposed into: preindustrial mean, preindustrial seasonal cycle, shifted decadal mean, shifted seasonal cycle, and residual (internal variability). Uncertainty = inter-model variance of each component.
- **Key result:** Absolute NPP uncertainty is quasi-stable (11.7 → 14.2 μgC m⁻² y⁻¹ under SSP5-8.5 by 2100; ~56–61% of multi-model mean), dominated by PI mean (59%) and PI seasonal cycle (26%) throughout. NPPA uncertainty roughly doubles on average, rising from ±1.3 to ±5.6 μgC m⁻² y⁻¹ by 2100 under SSP5-8.5, driven by shifted decadal mean (38% of NPPA uncertainty) and shifted seasonal cycle (33%) replacing residual variability as the dominant sources. NPPA signal-to-noise ratios reach only 0.31 globally even under SSP5-8.5, with S/N > 1 over <1% of ocean area; eliminating model uncertainty raises this to ~27% of area. SST anomaly S/N exceeds 1 over 93% of ocean area for comparison.
- **Why it matters:** Constraining historical NPP mean and seasonal cycle in ESMs is the highest-leverage action for reducing projection uncertainty; doing so could make climate-driven NPP changes detectable over key regions (Southern Ocean, North Atlantic, Benguela Upwelling) by 2100. Shifted seasonal cycle at high latitudes is newly identified as a major uncertainty driver, especially in the Arctic under sea-ice retreat.
- **Caveats:** Only 7 ESMs with all four SSPs available (12-ESM ensemble gives ~10% larger uncertainty but similar relative contributions). Low uncertainty does not guarantee accuracy — all tested ESMs fail to reproduce Argo/remote-sensing NPP trends in the Southern Ocean. Emergent-constraint potential for constraining projected shifts is inferred from PI-state correlations but not formally demonstrated.
