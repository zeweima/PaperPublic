# [Drinking Water Disinfection Using Nutritional Level Zinc Assisted with Electric Field Treatment](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.6c03361)

**Authors:** Wei Wang, Mourin Jarin, Farshid Khan et al.
**Venue:** Environmental Science & Technology · **Date:** 2026-04-29 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.6c03361
**Score:** 8/10 <sup>top pick</sup>
**Source:** abstract

## TL;DR

Electric field treatment combined with nutritional-level zinc (1.5 V, 3 mg/L) achieves effective microbial disinfection of natural surface water at 420 mL/min while simultaneously fortifying water with micronutrients.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Conventional drinking water disinfection methods lack dual functionality — they kill pathogens but do not address micronutrient deficiencies, and many are difficult to scale sustainably.
- **Method:** Electric field treatment (EFT) paired with zinc at nutritional concentrations (1.5 V, 3 mg/L Zn); tested at bench scale then scaled up to a flow-through system treating natural surface waters at 420 mL/min.
- **Key result:** Effective disinfection achieved at 1.5 V and 3 mg/L Zn; scaled system demonstrated practical throughput at 420 mL/min on real surface water matrices.
- **Why it matters:** EFT-Zn integrates microbial safety with zinc micronutrient fortification in a single step, potentially addressing both waterborne disease and nutritional deficiency in one low-cost, scalable treatment platform.
- **Caveats:** Abstract is sparse on quantitative log-reduction or pathogen-specific data; long-term performance, electrode fouling, and regulatory compliance for Zn levels in treated water are not addressed.
