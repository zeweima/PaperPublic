# [Physics‐AI Synergized Optimization‐Learning‐Simulation Framework for Robust Cascade Reservoir Scheduling Under Future Hydrological Uncertainty](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr042149)

**Authors:** Zhaoyang Zhu, Haotian Wu, Zhaocai Wang et al.
**Venue:** Water Resources Research · **Date:** 2026-05-01 · **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr042149

## TL;DR

Physics-informed LSTM with reinforcement learning and multi-objective optimization achieves NSE up to 0.96 for cascade reservoir scheduling across extreme hydrological scenarios.

## Summary
- **Problem:** Conventional hydropower scheduling lacks adaptability to uncertain future inflows; AI approaches struggle to bridge historical runoff-based prediction with operational physical constraints.
- **Method:** Optimization–Learning–Simulation (OLS) framework combining INSGA-III multi-objective optimization, VIKOR decision-making, physics-informed LSTM with reinforcement learning (PIRLSTM), SARIMA runoff forecasting, and Bootstrap/Cholesky inflow uncertainty simulation. Validated on four mega cascade reservoirs in the lower Jinsha River.
- **Key result:** NSE up to 0.96 and Water Balance Index near 1.00 across extreme dry, normal, and extreme wet years; framework dynamically adjusts scheduling for alternating wet–dry sequences and sudden drought-to-flood shifts.
- **Why it matters:** Provides a physically consistent, operationally applicable method for coordinated cascade reservoir management under climate-driven hydrological uncertainty — bridging theoretical AI innovation with engineering practice.
- **Caveats:** Validated on a single river basin (Jinsha River); generalizability to basins with different hydrology or governance structures untested. Training relies on historical runoff patterns that may not represent future non-stationarity.
