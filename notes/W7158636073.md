## TL;DR

ML framework integrating structural and electronic molecular features predicts organic pollutant reactivity (R²=0.811), enabling bottom-up design of advanced oxidation processes.

## Summary

- **Problem:** Predicting rate constants or reactivity of diverse organic pollutants in advanced oxidation processes (AOPs) is experimentally costly and lacks high-throughput approaches that capture both molecular structure and electronic properties.
- **Method:** Integrates structural molecular descriptors with electronic features into a machine learning framework; trained and validated on organic pollutant reactivity data; designed for high-throughput screening. Scale and specific ML algorithm are unclear from the available abstract fragment.
- **Key result:** Achieved R²=0.811 on reactivity prediction across organic pollutants; framework described as transparent and high-throughput.
- **Why it matters:** Enables bottom-up design of AOPs tailored to specific pollutant profiles, reducing experimental burden for water treatment and remediation applications.
- **Caveats:** Abstract is truncated — training set composition, pollutant classes covered, target oxidant(s), and external validation details are unclear from the available text.
