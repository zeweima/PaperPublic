## TL;DR

Global synthesis shows DCD, DMPP, and nitrapyrin nitrification inhibitors consistently cut N losses and improve N retention across agroecosystems.

## Summary
- **Problem:** Nitrogen losses from agroecosystems (leaching, denitrification, volatilization) degrade water quality and drive GHG emissions; comparative efficacy of the three main nitrification inhibitors (DCD, DMPP, nitrapyrin) across contexts is poorly synthesized.
- **Method:** Global meta-analysis synthesizing field trials with DCD, DMPP, and nitrapyrin applied across diverse agroecosystem types, climates, and N-fertilizer regimes.
