## TL;DR

Long-term conservation tillage in a semiarid dryland improves soil quality and wheat yield, with microbial communities and ecoenzymatic stoichiometry as mechanistic indicators.

- **Method:** Long-term field experiment (duration unclear from abstract) in a semiarid region; treatments compared conservation vs. conventional tillage; integrated soil microbial community analysis with ecoenzymatic stoichiometry to link biological activity to soil quality indices and wheat yield.
