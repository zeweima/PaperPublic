## Top picks

- [ ] **[Case‐Selective Dynamical Downscaling Enables a Tenfold Cost Reduction for Extreme Precipitation Statistics](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl122330)** · Dewettinck et al. · *Geophysical Research Letters* · [notes](../notes/W7160704549.md)

  > Simulating only extreme-rainfall windows (identified via coarse-resolution precipitation) rather than continuous convection-permitting runs reproduces 1–6 hourly extreme precipitation statistics at ~10% of the computational cost, with cases running in parallel. Validated against a full 30-year continuous simulation. Makes large-ensemble convection-permitting climate runs feasible for extreme-value statistics and storyline approaches.

- [ ] **[Heterogeneous Responses of High‐Latitude Forest Productivity to Interannual Climate Variability](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025gl121078)** · Liu et al. · *Geophysical Research Letters* · [notes](../notes/W7160704872.md)

  > SIF-based analysis reveals that warmer springs enhance carbon uptake across most high-latitude forests, but deciduous needleleaf forests show weak temperature sensitivity and strong water constraints — temperature–productivity relationships weaken or reverse in dry conditions. A single temperature-sensitivity parameter is insufficient; DNF water constraints must be explicitly represented in carbon cycle models to avoid overestimating warming-driven productivity gains.

- [ ] **[Regulating wheat yield, soil quality, and nitrous oxide emissions: Integrated effects of long-term straw mulching and nitrogen fertilization in dryland cropping systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.still.2026.107257)** · Yuan et al. · *Soil and Tillage Research* · [notes](../notes/W7160713918.md)

  > Long-term field experiment in dryland China testing combinations of straw mulching and N fertilization on winter wheat yield, soil quality, and N₂O fluxes. Identifies management combinations that co-optimize productivity, soil carbon, and GHG mitigation — directly relevant to sustainable dryland agriculture under N-use-efficiency and climate goals.

- [ ] **[Field Evidence of Carbon and Nitrogen Stabilization Through Mineral‐Associated Organic Matter Formation in Coastal Wetland Soils](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025jg009429)** · Mirabito et al. · *JGR Biogeosciences* · [notes](../notes/W7160691974.md)

  > MAOM comprises 38–66% of total C and >60% of total N in coastal wetland soils across two natural and one created wetland in Florida. The created wetland stabilized 44–65% of total C and 68–84% of total N within MAOM — higher fractions than natural wetlands despite lower absolute concentrations — with MAOM better predicted by organic soil properties than by mineral properties. Supports wetland restoration as a rapid, efficient carbon sequestration strategy.

- [ ] **[Informing Robust Functional Relationship Benchmarks: An Evaluation of the Temperature Sensitivity of Ecosystem Respiration Across the Arctic‐Boreal Region](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025jg009307)** · Poe et al. · *JGR Biogeosciences* · [notes](../notes/W7160697582.md)

  > Benchmark construction choices — number of daily observations and temporal extent — drive larger differences in inferred land model skill than the choice of observational dataset itself, across 13 WrPMIP models over Alaska and Canada (2001–2020). Provides reproducible guidelines for constructing functional relationship benchmarks that reduce spurious variation in permafrost carbon cycle model evaluation.

- [ ] **[Physics‐AI Synergized Optimization‐Learning‐Simulation Framework for Robust Cascade Reservoir Scheduling Under Future Hydrological Uncertainty](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr042149)** · Zhu et al. · *Water Resources Research* · [notes](../notes/W7160707333.md)

  > OLS framework combining INSGA-III multi-objective optimization, physics-informed LSTM with reinforcement learning (PIRLSTM), and SARIMA runoff forecasting achieves NSE up to 0.96 and Water Balance Index near 1.00 across extreme dry/normal/wet scenarios on four Jinsha River cascade reservoirs. Dynamically adjusts scheduling for alternating wet–dry sequences and sudden drought-to-flood shifts, providing a physically consistent operational method for climate-driven reservoir management.

- [ ] **[Exotic Plant Invasion Enhances Nitrous Oxide Emission and Methane Uptake in Forests and Grasslands: A Global Meta‐Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025ef007893)** · He et al. · *Earth's Future* · [notes](../notes/W7160712009.md)

  > Global meta-analysis (141 paired observations) finds exotic plant invasion raises temperate grassland N₂O by +197% (1.35 → 4.01 kg N₂O ha⁻¹ yr⁻¹) and increases tropical/subtropical forest CH₄ uptake by +30%, yielding a net warming forcing of 59.7 ± 21.1 Tg CO₂ eq yr⁻¹. Invasion-driven N₂O enhancement is a largely unaccounted GHG source that will grow as climate facilitates invasion spread.

- [ ] **[Spatiotemporal Machine Learning Approaches for Atmospheric Composition Emulation in NASA GISS ModelE](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025jh001011)** · Erfani et al. · *JGR Machine Learning and Computation* · [notes](../notes/W7160709159.md)

  > ConvLSTM-based "Smart-NINT" emulator replaces explicit tracer transport in NASA GISS ModelE, achieving R² ~0.80 for surface black carbon while restoring meteorological feedbacks lost in static climatologies. A Smart-NINT coupling layer could enable longer or higher-resolution climate projections at a fraction of full tracer transport cost.

- [ ] **[Beyond GSD-as-Token: Continuous Scale Conditioning for Remote Sensing VLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07562)** · Zhang et al. · *arXiv (cs.CV)* · [notes](../notes/arxiv-2605.07562.md)

  > ScaleEarth (QLoRA-finetuned 8B Qwen3-VL) adds CS-HLoRA which modulates LoRA subspaces via a GSD-driven gate for dynamic scale routing, enabling scale-aware RS interpretation without requiring sensor metadata at inference. Achieves state-of-the-art on XLRS-Bench and OmniEarth-Bench, closing the mismatch between multi-resolution RS imagery and fixed-scale VLMs.

- [ ] **[Cloud-top infrared observations reveal the four-dimensional precipitation structure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07499)** · Xu et al. · *arXiv (cs.CV)* · [notes](../notes/arxiv-2605.07499.md)

  > 4DPrecipNet uses geostationary IR cloud-top radiances with a physics-constrained moisture-first deep learning framework to reconstruct full 4D precipitation fields, establishing that sub-cloud precipitation structure is physically encoded in cloud-top IR. Opens a path to continuous global 4D precipitation monitoring from existing geostationary satellites with no additional hardware.

- [ ] **[Dust Decline Amplifies High‐Cloud Ice‐to‐Liquid Transition and Buffers the Radiative Feedback Under Warming](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl121917)** · Wang et al. · *Geophysical Research Letters* · [notes](../notes/W7160714960.md)

  > Satellite comparison of Northern vs. Southern Hemisphere high clouds shows that declining mineral dust accelerates ice-to-liquid conversion in the NH, creating a radiative cooling buffer that offsets 25% of the net positive cloud feedback from warming. Ignoring this hemisphere-dependent aerosol–cloud coupling introduces systematic bias into climate sensitivity estimates.

- [ ] **[Organic amendments induce crop-specific soil biotic homogenization associated with SOC sequestration: Evidence from a 35-year intercropping experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.still.2026.107255)** · Zhang et al. · *Soil and Tillage Research* · [notes](../notes/W7160706115.md)

  > 35-year field intercropping experiment demonstrates that organic amendments drive crop-specific convergence of soil biotic communities, and this homogenization is mechanistically linked to SOC gains. Relevant for designing amendment strategies in intercropping systems seeking both productivity and soil carbon co-benefits.

- [ ] **[On the Construction of Multivariate Drought Indices: Theoretical Foundations and Practical Implications](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr041665)** · De Michele et al. · *Water Resources Research* · [notes](../notes/W7160690988.md)

  > Theoretical derivation shows that correct multivariate Gaussian normalization of drought indices requires the Kendall distribution function — omitting this correction biases drought occurrence frequency estimates. Many copula-based multivariate drought index implementations (e.g., MSDI, multivariate SPI variants) implicitly apply the incorrect standardization.

- [ ] **[A Socio‐Hydrological Model for Adaptive Water Allocation in the Heihe River Basin](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025wr040623)** · Han et al. · *Water Resources Research* · [notes](../notes/W7160706336.md)

  > Coupled socio-hydrological model with human decision-making, hydrological, economic, and ecological subsystems reproduces 70 years of water allocation history in the Heihe Basin; identifies two distinct time lags in the ecological subsystem (delayed recognition of deterioration, delayed biophysical recovery) that explain the 1990s–2000s economic-ecological trade-off. Formalizing adaptive decision dynamics improves predictability of sustainable basin management strategies.

- [ ] **[Global Occurrence, Drivers, and Ecological Risk of Pesticide Contamination in Lakes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2026.126094)** · Ayiera et al. · *Water Research* · [notes](../notes/W7160711132.md)

  > Global synthesis characterizing pesticide occurrence patterns, environmental drivers, and ecological risk in lakes. Relevant for prioritizing monitoring and regulatory action on pesticide inputs to freshwater systems; limited detail on methods and results available from abstract.
