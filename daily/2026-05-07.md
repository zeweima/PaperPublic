**Subsurface soil inorganic carbon gains offset half of surface losses in China's upland croplands over the last four decades**
Cai et al. · PNAS · 2026-05-05
[https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2531214123](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2531214123) · [notes](../notes/W7160270277.md)

Paired-site resampling at 204 locations (4,305 profiles) shows surface SIC (0–40 cm) declined 0.68 kg m⁻² over four decades driven by acidification and increased precipitation, while subsurface SIC (40–100 cm) gained 0.49 kg m⁻² (0.48 Pg total), offsetting 44% of surface losses. **Temperature‐Driven Silicate Weathering Feedbacks Terminated the Middle Eocene Climatic Optimum**
Ma et al. · Geophysical Research Letters · 2026-05-05
[https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl121765](https://doi.org/10.1029/2026gl121765) · [notes](../notes/W7160258502.md)

High-resolution multi-proxy records from four East Asian lake basins show that before and during early MECO (~40 Ma), intensified precipitation drove chemical weathering and physical erosion; at peak MECO, chemical weathering peaked despite reduced rainfall, indicating a shift to temperature-controlled weathering.
