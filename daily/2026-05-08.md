### Climate & global change

**Deforestation-induced drying lowers Amazon climate threshold**
Wunderling et al. · *Nature* · 2026-05-06
[https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10456-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10456-0) · [notes](../notes/W7160440433.md)

Without deforestation the Amazon's critical warming threshold is 3.7–4.0 °C with ~33% of forest losing stability; at 22–28% deforestation the threshold collapses to 1.5–1.9 °C and 62–77% of forest area faces near system-wide transition. The majority of transitions propagate via spatial drought cascades operating over hundreds to thousands of km. Keeping warming below 1.5 °C is insufficient alone — simultaneous deforestation halt and forest restoration are required to avoid high transition probabilities; the study shows climate targets and land-use policy are deeply coupled for Amazon resilience.
