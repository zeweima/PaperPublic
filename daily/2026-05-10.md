- [ ] **[Global Vegetation Greening Is Exacerbating Soil Dryness](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.70901)** · Z. Qu et al. · *Global Change Biology* · [notes](../notes/W7160639763.md)

  > Vegetation greening amplifies soil dryness across 59–71% of vegetated land globally (1982–2020), driven by rising transpiration dominance over precipitation inputs. Earth system models poorly reproduce — and often distort — the temporal evolution of this vegetation-driven soil drying, meaning water resource projections under greening scenarios are likely systematically optimistic. Vegetation-driven soil drying represents an underaccounted positive feedback in the hydrological cycle.

- [ ] **[Capturing Spatiotemporal and Subgrid Variability in Global Land Surface Albedo Parameterization](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025ms005436)** · Akarsh Ralhan et al. · *JAMES* · [notes](../notes/W7160660162.md)
