### Hydrology & water resources

**What can hydrological modelling gain from spatially explicit parameterization and multi-gauge calibration?**
Zheng et al. · HESS · 2026-04-29
[https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-30-2493-2026](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-30-2493-2026) · [notes](../notes/W7159614927.md)

VIC-5 + multiscale parameter regionalization (MPR) tested across 8 calibration configurations (uniform vs. distributed parameterization × single vs. multi-gauge) in the Upper Han River Basin (~23 000 km²). Combined distributed parameterization + multi-gauge calibration (NSGA-II, KGE at 5 gauges) ranks first or second at all sub-basins: calibration KGE up to 0.787, validation KGE up to 0.745. Spatially explicit parameterization alone improves high-flow bias by 18% but degrades low-flow performance by 8.6%; multi-gauge calibration tightens parameter posteriors and enables robust transfer to ungauged upstream sub-basins with negligible KGE loss. Under uniform parameterization, multi-gauge objectives form a hard convex Pareto ceiling — spatially explicit parameterization breaks this ceiling by expanding the feasible solution space. Open-source Python EVB framework released.

### Land-surface process & modeling

**Impact of soil heterogeneity and lateral heat fluxes on soil temperature simulations in a permafrost-affected soil**
Thurner, Rodriguez-Lloveras, Beer · Geoscientific Model Development · 2026-04-29
[https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-19-3509-2026](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-19-3509-2026) · [notes](../notes/W7159660831.md)

New 2D pedon-scale model DynSoM-2D (10 cm resolution) applied at a non-sorted circle site in East Siberia. Heterogeneous soil warms +0.19 °C (no lateral flux) to +0.25 °C (with lateral conduction) relative to a homogeneous LSM-like reference; daily topsoil biases reach 5 °C in summer. Active layer deepens 10–20 cm; snow-free period extends up to 5 days. Annual soil-atmosphere heat budget differences are <1 W m⁻². The aggregation error traces to nonlinear dependence of thermal conductivity on organic matter content — OM-rich patches lower mean κ more than an even OM distribution would. Lateral heat fluxes reduce spatial spread but amplify domain-mean warming. Results imply standard LSMs systematically underestimate permafrost temperatures, with downstream consequences for active layer depth and C-cycle projections.
